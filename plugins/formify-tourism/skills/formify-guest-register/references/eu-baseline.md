# EU baseline: guest registration

What holds in every EU country, read on the primary texts on 2026-11-26. Sources and the sentences to
re-check are in `controls.json`. The EU level is thin: it sets who must register and sign, and leaves
the data list, retention, form and filing to each country. The country layer wins over this file
wherever it says something different.

## 1. The Schengen duty (Convention, art. 45)

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accommodation managers or their agents see to it that foreign guests, including nationals of other EU states, personally complete and sign registration forms and confirm their identity by producing a valid identity document. | art. 45(1)(a) |
| 2 | Accompanying spouses, accompanying minors and members of travel groups are exempt from completing and signing. The text does not say what happens to their data. | art. 45(1)(a) |
| 3 | The duty names foreigners. A guest who is a national of the host state is not covered at EU level; national law decides. | art. 45(1)(a) |
| 4 | Completed forms are kept for the competent authorities or forwarded to them where they deem it necessary, unless national law provides otherwise. No retention period is set. | art. 45(1)(b) |
| 5 | The duty extends to any commercially rented accommodation, such as tents, caravans and boats. | art. 45(2) |
| 6 | The article says nothing about paper or electronic form, electronic signatures, copies of the ID, retention or what is sent to the police. Say "the EU text is silent"; never say electronic registration is accepted EU-wide. | whole article |

The article is a duty on the states to adopt measures, so a country may go further: cover its own
nationals, require more data, set retention, choose the form and the channel. No act repealing or rewriting art. 45 was found on 2026-11-26;
that is an absence of evidence, not a confirmation. Which states apply the Schengen acquis in
full (Ireland, Cyprus) was not checked: do not say "all 27".

## 2. Personal data (GDPR)

National authority positions on ID copies (Spain, Greece, Italy) were seen only in secondary sources
and are not stated as fact.

## 3. Short-term rental data (Regulation (EU) 2024/1028)

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Applies since 20 May 2026. | art. 18 |
| 13 | Hotels and similar accommodation, hostels and camping grounds are outside the "unit" definition. It covers private hosts offering through online platforms. | art. 3(1), 3(2) |
| 14 | A host offering through a platform declares whether the unit is subject to registration and gives the registration number; the number is shown on the listing. | art. 4(3)(h), 7(1)(b) |
| 15 | It creates declarations from host to authority and data flows from platform to authority. It creates no document that host and guest sign. | art. 5, 9 |

Whether a country or city requires a registration number is national, and the procedure is not part
of this skill.

## 4. Signature (eIDAS)

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | An electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect or admissibility as evidence only because it is electronic. | Reg. (EU) 910/2014 art. 25(1) |
| 17 | A qualified electronic signature has the legal effect of a handwritten one. | art. 25(2) |
| 18 | Whether a document needs handwriting, a qualified signature or a notary is national law. | art. 2(3) |

Rows 6 and 16 together: the signature is admissible, but whether a country accepts an electronic
registration form in place of paper is a national question. Where a country says paper or handwriting
is required, deliver that document to print and sign.

## 5. What the baseline can promise

1. Foreign guests, including other EU nationals, complete and sign personally; accompanying spouses,
   minors and travel-group members are exempt from signing.
2. The same applies to tents, caravans and boats rented commercially.
3. The EU text says nothing on electronic form, ID copies, retention or filing with the police.
4. Collect only what the law requires, keep it no longer than needed, and by default keep no ID image.
5. Hosts on online platforms may owe a registration number to the platform; hotels and campsites are
   outside that regulation. No host-guest signed document comes from it.
6. A booking with a fixed date has no EU withdrawal right under the consumer rights directive, but that
   concerns the booking contract, which this skill does not produce.
