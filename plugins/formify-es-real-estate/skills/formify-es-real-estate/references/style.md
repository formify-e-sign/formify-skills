# Spanish contract drafting style (applies to BOTH columns)

How to use this file: read it before writing any free text of your own (party formulas, optional phrases, the invitation message, and every translated column). Fixed clause text in the templates already follows it; do not "improve" that text. The translated column is a legal translation in the same register, not an explanation. Then run `scripts/check_style.py` on the finished HTML.

Non-negotiable rules from the Formify house style: no dashes as punctuation (no " - ", no en or em dash as a pause; split the sentence or use a colon), no phrases that read as generated text ("es importante destacar", "cabe señalar"), no bold on random keywords, no mention of AI or disclaimers inside a document.

# Style guide: drafting private contracts in Spanish (Spain)

Scope: private civil/commercial contracts (compraventa, arras, mediación/hoja de encargo, arrendamiento, colaboración). Goal: text that reads as drafted by a Spanish lawyer or estate agency, not as a translation or AI output. Sources are cited per section and listed in §11. Items marked **[unverified]** are practice observations I could not confirm in an authoritative source.
