# Term list: Spanish property terms that are NOT translated

Rule: in the translated column, keep the Spanish term the first time it appears and add a short gloss in the client's language in parentheses; afterwards use the Spanish term alone or the gloss consistently. These are legal institutions with no exact equivalent; a translated word would mislead the client and weaken the Spanish master text. For languages not listed, follow the same rule and gloss in that language.
