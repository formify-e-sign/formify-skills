## Rule that does not change

Every block with bank, insurer, guarantee or register data is offered with a question and the user decides; the table above is context for phrasing the question well, never for imposing the block or for saying in the chat that something is mandatory.

## What changed in this check (compared with version 2.0.1)

- Andalucía has a register that is mandatory by law since 24-01-2026 (Ley 5/2025), although nobody can register yet; the mandate note must already carry the policy and insurer, or the guarantee and its provider (art. 53.3). We used to say "no register".
- Canarias (RAIC, 2022) and Navarra (2019) have had a voluntary register for years. We used to say "no register".
- Extremadura, País Vasco and Murcia have the register written into their housing law but never developed it; Extremadura also makes liability insurance mandatory by law.
- Comunitat Valenciana: registration is still mandatory, but the guarantee and insurance amounts in Decreto 98/2022 are annulled (STSJCV 706/2024).
- Navarra has its own arras rule (Fuero Nuevo ley 467): without an express agreement the arras are confirmatory, as in Cataluña. New variant FN in arras and reservation.
- Baleares has its own family home rule (Compilación art. 4.3 and 67.1, since Ley 7/2017), not art. 1320 CC. The family home sentence in the arras cites the Compilación.
- País Vasco: Ley 5/2015 has no family home rule (its art. 12 is about the "caserío" farmstead); the Código Civil applies.
- IGIC 7 % with a new citation (Decreto Legislativo 1/2025 art. 32.1; Ley 4/2012 is repealed); IPSI 4 % in Ceuta and in Melilla for services.
- Consumer law (TRLGDCU) art. 60.2.c and 20.1.c: the consumer is informed of the total price including taxes; the fee row of the mandate note now prints the total with the tax included. Art. 60.4: the pre-contractual information is also drawn up, at the request of either party, in any of the other official languages of the place of the contract; this applies in the six regions with a co-official language.
- Language: Cataluña is the only region where the consumer has the right to receive the contract in the co-official language (art. 128-1); in Baleares, Valencia, Galicia and País Vasco they have the right to use it and to information on request; in Navarra there is no consumer rule.

## Default profile

- Official: Baleares, Cataluña and Comunitat Valenciana for mandate and KYC (the administration has fixed the form).
- Professional: the rest, and arras and collaboration everywhere.
- Light: reservation at a viewing, collaboration from the phone, keys.
The user can always change it; the choice is saved in the memory.
