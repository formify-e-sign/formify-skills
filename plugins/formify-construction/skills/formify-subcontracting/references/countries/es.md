# Spain (ES): subcontracting between a main contractor and a subcontractor

Verified pack. Every row was read on the official consolidated text on 2026-11-26; the links and
the sentences to re-check are in `../controls.json`. Ley 32/2006 was last updated 2025-12-18 and
Real Decreto 1109/2007 on 2025-12-19, as shown on the BOE pages. Language of the document: Spanish
as master text, with the other party's language as a second column. No language rule for these
business documents was checked.

## 1. Registration: the REA

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Companies must be entered in the Registro de Empresas Acreditadas (REA) | Required to be contracted or subcontracted for works on a construction site | Ley 32/2006 art. 4.2.b; RD 1109/2007 art. 3.1 |
| Who declares | The legal representative of the company, to the REA; entry is made by the labour authority. This is not a document between the two businesses | Ley 32/2006 art. 4.3 |
| Validity | Nationwide, with public access to the data; three years, renewable | Ley 32/2006 art. 6.2; RD 1109/2007 art. 6.1 |
| Certificate for the contractor | The contracting company obtains a certificate of the subcontractor's entry. The request must be made within the month before execution starts, and the certificate is issued within ten natural days | RD 1109/2007 art. 6.3-6.4 |
| Effect of the certificate | The contracting company is taken to have met its duty of supervision and is relieved, for the term of the contract and for one works, of the liability in Ley 32/2006 art. 7.2 | RD 1109/2007 art. 6.3 |

Whether the particular works fall within the Spanish definition of a construction works for this
law was not checked. Say so when it matters, and let the contractor decide.

## 2. The chain of subcontractors

Ley 32/2006 art. 5.1 says subcontracting may not be limited except in the cases the law states.
Art. 5.2 states them:

| Who | May subcontract onward? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The developer (promotor) | Contracts directly with as many contractors as it chooses | art. 5.2.a |
| The contractor | May contract subcontractors or self-employed workers | art. 5.2.b |
| First and second subcontractor | May subcontract | art. 5.2.c |
| Third subcontractor | May not subcontract the work contracted with another subcontractor or self-employed worker | art. 5.2.d |
| Self-employed worker (autónomo) | May not subcontract the work entrusted to them, neither to companies nor to other self-employed workers. Being contracted by the contractor is allowed | art. 5.2.e |
| Subcontractor whose productive organisation on site is essentially the supply of labour | May not subcontract | art. 5.2.f |

Effect on block J of the subcontract: print what the parties state about onward subcontracting and
say, in one sentence, which row above applies to the subcontractor. Do not pick the answer for them.

**The exceptional extra tier (named, not produced).** Art. 5.3 allows one additional level
exceptionally, if the site management (dirección facultativa) records its prior approval and the
reasons in the Libro de Subcontratación. It is not available for the self-employed worker or the
labour-only subcontractor except for force majeure. Art. 5.4 asks the contractor to inform the
safety coordinator and the workers' representatives and to send the labour authority, within five
working days of the approval, a report with the circumstances and a copy of the entry. The text of
art. 5.3 refers to the book as "artículo 7"; the book is in art. 8. This skill produces neither the
approval nor the report.

**The Libro de Subcontratación (named, not produced).** Art. 8.1: each contractor must hold one,
kept on site at all times, recording every subcontracting in chronological order from the start of
the works. RD 1109/2007 arts. 13 to 16 and its Anexo III cover how it is kept and its model. A
signed subcontract does not replace the entry.

## 3. Liability and the certificates to collect

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Breach of the REA or tier rules | Joint liability of the subcontractor that contracted in breach and of its contractor, for the labour and Social Security obligations arising from the contract. It is a liability for labour and Social Security only | Ley 32/2006 art. 7.2 |
| Assignment of workers | Liability under art. 43 of the Estatuto de los Trabajadores where its conditions are met (art. 43, not art. 42) | Ley 32/2006 art. 7.3 |
| Social Security certificate | A company that contracts or subcontracts works or services of its own activity must check that the contractor is up to date with Social Security contributions, and requests in writing, naming the company, a negative certificate of arrears from the Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social | Estatuto de los Trabajadores art. 42.1 |
| Length of that liability | Social Security: three years after the end of the assignment. Wages: the year after | ET art. 42.2 |
| Tax certificate | The payer is not liable, in the subsidiary way the law describes, for the tax debts of a contractor or subcontractor who gave it a specific certificate of being up to date, issued by the tax authority during the 12 months before the payment of each invoice. Liability is limited to payments made without the certificate. The tax authority issues or denies it within three days of the request | Ley General Tributaria art. 43.1.f |

ET art. 42 and LGT art. 43.1.f are separate regimes from Ley 32/2006 art. 7. Never describe one as
part of the other.

**Document 5, the checklist for Spain.** Three lines, each with who requests it and validity as above:

| Line | Requested by | Validity as the law gives it |
|---|---|---|
| REA registration certificate | The contractor, within the month before execution | Issued within ten natural days; protects for one works and the contract term |
| Social Security certificate (no arrears) | The contractor, in writing, from the Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social | Not stated in the rows read; say it was not checked |
| Tax certificate (up to date) | The subcontractor supplies it to the contractor as payer | Issued within the 12 months before each invoice payment |

Whether a given certificate is still in force is for the contractor to check with the issuing body.

## 4. Payment between the two businesses

Ley 3/2004 (consolidated, last updated 2025-12-18), art. 4 in the text in force since 2013-07-28:

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No date or period fixed | 30 natural days after receipt of the goods or services | art. 4.1 |
| Acceptance or verification procedure | At most 30 natural days | art. 4.2 |
| Periods agreed by the parties | May be extended by agreement, but never above 60 natural days | art. 4.3 |
| Grouped invoices | Payment no later than 60 days | art. 4.4 |
| Scope | Commercial transactions between companies, including those between main contractors and their suppliers and subcontractors | art. 3.1 |
| Excluded | Payments in transactions in which consumers take part | art. 3.2.a |

Effect on block D: print the period the parties state. When it is above 60 natural days, say once
that art. 4.3 allows no period above 60 natural days, with no exception, and let the parties decide.
When an acceptance period is above 30 days, say the same with art. 4.2. Never suggest a period.

Ley 15/2010 changed Ley 3/2004; no rule for private construction was found in it. Its construction
item concerns public works and is out of scope. The interest rate and compensation in arts. 5 to 8
of Ley 3/2004 were not checked: leave the rate to the parties and do not state a figure.

## 5. Signature

| Point | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form of the subcontract | Neither Ley 32/2006 nor RD 1109/2007 imposes a form, in the texts read. Contracts bind whatever the form, if their essential conditions are present | Código Civil art. 1278 |
| Electronic document | Electronic private documents have the value and legal effect that corresponds to their nature. This is a rule on documents and evidence, not a rule that an electronic signature equals a handwritten one | Ley 6/2020 art. 3 |
| Electronic signature | Cannot be denied effect only because it is electronic | Regulation (EU) 910/2014 art. 25(1) |

None of the provisions read asks for a qualified electronic signature or a notary for these five
documents. Formify's e-signature is used for all of them.
