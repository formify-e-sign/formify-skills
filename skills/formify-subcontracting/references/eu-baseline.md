# EU baseline: subcontracting between two businesses

What holds in every EU country, read on the EU texts on 2026-11-26. Sources and the sentences to
re-check are in `controls.json`. "LPD" is Directive 2011/7/EU on combating late payment in
commercial transactions, original text, which has no amendments.

A country can be stricter. The country layer wins over this file wherever it says something
different.

## 1. Payment between businesses

| # | Rule | Effect on the document | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Between undertakings the creditor is entitled to interest for late payment without the necessity of a reminder, when it has fulfilled its obligations and has not received the amount on time. | Never write a clause that needs a reminder before interest runs. | LPD art. 3(1) |
| 2 | When the contract fixes no period, the default is 30 calendar days after receipt of the invoice or an equivalent request for payment. Where an acceptance or verification procedure is provided for, it is 30 calendar days after acceptance, if the invoice came earlier or on that date. | Offered only as the rule that applies when the parties state nothing. Never suggested as their term. | LPD art. 3(3)(b) |
| 3 | A period fixed in the contract may not exceed 60 calendar days, unless expressly agreed in the contract and provided it is not grossly unfair to the creditor. | The period the parties state is printed. When it is above 60 calendar days, say so once and let them decide. | LPD art. 3(5) |
| 4 | An acceptance or verification procedure may not exceed 30 calendar days from receipt of the goods or services, unless expressly agreed in the contract and provided it is not grossly unfair to the creditor. | When the parties state an acceptance period above 30 days, say so once, the same way. | LPD art. 3(4) |
| 5 | The creditor is entitled to a fixed sum of at least EUR 40 as recovery costs, without a reminder, plus reasonable compensation for costs above that. | Do not print a clause that waives it. | LPD art. 6(1)-(3) |
| 6 | A term excluding interest for late payment is considered grossly unfair; a term excluding recovery-cost compensation is presumed grossly unfair. | Do not write either clause. | LPD art. 7(2)-(3) |
| 7 | A term on the payment date or period, the interest rate or recovery costs is unenforceable or gives rise to a claim for damages if it is grossly unfair to the creditor. | Same. | LPD art. 7(1) |
| 8 | Member States may keep or adopt provisions more favourable to the creditor, so national limits can be shorter than rows 2 to 4. | The country layer decides; ask or look up. | LPD art. 12(3) |
| 9 | A proposed Late Payment Regulation (COM(2023)533) is pending: not adopted and not withdrawn as of 2026-11-26. | Directive 2011/7/EU is the applicable EU law. Never cite the proposal's figures as law and never predict its outcome. | Legislative Observatory, procedure 2023/0323(COD) |

The statutory interest rate was not read on the text: leave the rate to the parties and the
country layer.

## 2. Liability along the chain

| # | Rule | Effect on the document | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | For building work, a Member State must provide that posted workers can hold the contractor of which their employer is a direct subcontractor liable, or provide other appropriate enforcement measures with effective and proportionate sanctions against the contractor. | The subcontract may let the contractor demand supporting documents. Which ones is national. | Directive 2014/67/EU art. 12(2), 12(6) |
| 11 | Stricter national rules are allowed, and a contractor that has met the due diligence obligations defined by national law is not liable. What counts as due diligence is national. | Do not say what protects the contractor without the country layer. | Directive 2014/67/EU art. 12(4)-(5) |

The EU imposes no document on the parties here. A posting declaration to the authority of the host
state is a national administrative requirement and is not produced by this skill.

## 3. Site safety and health

| # | Rule | Effect on the document | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | For a site where more than one contractor is present, the client or the project supervisor appoints one or more safety and health coordinators, and ensures a safety and health plan is drawn up before the site is set up. | The duty lies on the client or project supervisor, not on the subcontractor. | Directive 92/57/EEC art. 3(1)-(2) |
| 13 | The execution coordinator ensures that employers and self-employed persons follow the plan where required, and employers take into account the coordinator's directions. | Offer a clause that the subcontractor follows the site plan and the coordinator's directions. The EU text does not require it. | Directive 92/57/EEC arts. 6(b), 9(b) |

Thresholds, who counts as client, the content of the plan and prior-notice forms are national.

## 5. Signature

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | An electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect or admissibility as evidence only because it is electronic. | Regulation (EU) 910/2014 art. 25(1) |
| 16 | A qualified electronic signature has the legal effect of a handwritten one. | Regulation (EU) 910/2014 art. 25(2) |
| 17 | Whether a document needs handwriting, a qualified signature or a notary is national law. | Regulation (EU) 910/2014 art. 2(3) |

Formify signatures are covered by row 15. Where a country asks for the form in row 16 or for
handwriting, say so and do not send that document for e-signature.

## 6. What EU law does not set

Retention percentages, payment certificates, bank guarantees, direct claims of the subcontractor
against the client, the depth of a subcontracting chain, registers and licences of construction
firms, the certificates a contractor must collect, reverse-charge VAT, acceptance and defect
periods and standard construction terms were not found as EU rules. They are national: ask or look
up (`country-lookup.md`). Where nothing was checked, write that it was not checked.
