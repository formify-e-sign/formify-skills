# Spain, region by region

Read the row for the **property's** autonomous community before drafting anything. Not the
agency's region: the property's.

Every row was checked against the consolidated BOE text or the region's own gazette on
**8 September 2026**. Anything that could not be read in a primary source is marked
`[unverified]`. Where a row says "no register", that is a checked absence, not an assumption.

If the user says their region has just created a register, believe them, ask for the name and
the number, and use it.

## The rule that does not change

Every block carrying bank, insurer, guarantee or register data is **offered as a question and
the user decides**. The table above is context for asking the question well. It is never a
reason to impose a block, and never a reason to tell the user in the chat that something is
mandatory.

## The register line in the header and in box A

- Registered: *"&lt;register name from the table&gt;, n.º &lt;number&gt;"*.
- Not registered where registration is **voluntary** (Baleares, Madrid, Canarias, Navarra):
  *"No inscrito (inscripción voluntaria en esta región)"*.
- Not registered where registration is **mandatory and operative** (Cataluña, Comunitat
  Valenciana): *"Pendiente de inscripción"*, plus one sentence in the chat — without
  registering they cannot legally offer the property. Once, and no more.
- Andalucía: *"Inscripción pendiente de desarrollo reglamentario (Ley 5/2025)"*, no chat note.
- Regions with no register: the line is not printed at all.

## The family home: who signs, and which article the declaration cites

Disposing of the family's habitual home needs both spouses' consent even when only one owns it.
In an **arras** contract the non-owning spouse signs. In an **encargo** only the owner signs;
the spouse is optional and worth offering under an exclusive mandate.

| Where | Article the declaration cites |
|---|---|
| Everywhere not listed below, including Comunitat Valenciana, País Vasco and Galicia | Código Civil art. 1320 |
| Cataluña | CCCat art. 231-9 — consent cannot be given in general terms; a sale without it is voidable for four years |
| Illes Balears | Compilación art. 4.3 (Mallorca, Menorca) or art. 67.1 (Eivissa, Formentera), voidable for four years |
| Aragón | CDFA art. 190 |
| Navarra | Fuero Nuevo ley 81 |

If the property is **not** the family home, the arras contract still carries the declaration
that it is not, citing the same article.

## Tax on the fee, and how it is printed

IVA 21 % on the mainland and the Balearics — there is no reduced rate for agency work. IGIC 7 %
in the Canaries. IPSI 4 % in Ceuta and Melilla.

The consumer must see the **total including tax**. The fee row prints the percentage or amount,
the tax with its rate, and the total with tax included: *"el 5 % más IVA"* is also printed as
*"6,05 % del precio, IVA incluido"*. Never *"IVA no incluido"* on its own.

## Statutory minimum content of the nota de encargo

**Illes Balears.** a) client's details and the title that entitles them; b) the agent: name and
address, company, address and CIF, register number if registered; c) the property with its
registry identification and cadastral reference; d) the transaction, stating expressly whether
it is exclusive; e) a term with start and end, or indefinite until revoked with the mechanism
for revoking; f) price or rent; g) fees broken down by tax and how they are paid; h) the
indemnity policy and the guarantee. **The minimum content must be visibly separate from the
freely agreed terms** — which is why the template has boxes A to D and the stipulations in E.

**Cataluña.** a) the agent's identity and AICAT number; b) the owners' identity and their
representative if any; c) the term; d) the transaction; e) the property with registry data,
charges, encumbrances and affectations; f) any protected-housing regime; g) the asking price;
h) relevant legal aspects, in particular pending court proceedings, declared under the owner's
responsibility; i) the agent's fee (percentage or fixed) and how it is paid — **it may not be
charged to both parties** unless expressly agreed; j) the parties' rights and obligations and
the agent's powers. The mandate is signed **before** the property is offered or advertised, and
the agent verifies title and charges before signing anything with third parties.

**Comunitat Valenciana.** The RAICV registration number and badge on contracts and advertising;
the insurer or bank and the guarantee's reference number. The annulled minimum figures are not
printed.

**Andalucía.** The insurer's name and policy number, or the bank and guarantee number, must be
given to interested parties on request **and included in the mandate**.

**Everywhere else.** No regional list. Consumer pre-contractual information applies — the
trader's identity and tax number, the total price including tax, the duration, the contract
language, the right of withdrawal — and the template is used in full anyway. Over-complying is
cheaper than under-complying.

## What changed at the 8 September 2026 check

- **Andalucía** has a legally mandatory register since 24-01-2026, though nobody can register
  yet; the mandate must already carry the policy or guarantee. Previously recorded as "no
  register".
- **Canarias** (2022) and **Navarra** (2019) have had voluntary registers for years. Previously
  recorded as "no register".
- **Extremadura, País Vasco and Murcia** wrote a register into their housing law and never
  developed it; Extremadura also makes indemnity insurance mandatory by law.
- **Comunitat Valenciana**: registration is still mandatory, but the guarantee and insurance
  figures are annulled.
- **Navarra** has its own arras rule (Fuero Nuevo ley 467): without an express agreement, arras
  are confirmatorias, as in Cataluña. This is a distinct variant.
- **Illes Balears** has its own family-home rule in the Compilación, not art. 1320 CC.
- **País Vasco**: Ley 5/2015 has no family-home rule; the Código Civil governs.
- Consumer law requires the **total price including tax**, so the fee row now prints it.
  Pre-contractual information must also be drawn up, at either party's request, in any of the
  other official languages of the place of contract — which applies in all six regions with a
  co-official language.
- **Language**: Cataluña is the only region where the consumer has a *right* to receive the
  contract in the co-official language. In Baleares, Valencia, Galicia and País Vasco they have
  a right to use it and to information on request. Navarra has no consumer rule.
