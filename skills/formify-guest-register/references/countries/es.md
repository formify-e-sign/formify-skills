# Spain (ES): guest registration

Verified pack. Every row was read on the official text on 2026-11-26; the links and the sentences to
re-check are in `../controls.json`. The source is Real Decreto 933/2021 (RD 933/2021), consolidated
text of 2025-12-20, with no amendment since publication. Language of the document: Spanish; offer the
guest's language as a second column. The Annex I labels stay in Spanish beside the translation.

## 1. Who is covered

| Situation | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Anyone who lodges people for a price, professionally or not: hotels, hostels, guesthouses, rural tourism | Covered | art. 1, 2.1, 2.1.a |
| Campsites, motorhome areas, apartments, bungalows and similar tourist lodging, whoever owns them | Covered | art. 2.1.b |
| Hostels (albergues) where a price is paid | Covered | Interior FAQ of 10/08/2026, Q17 |
| University residences used for housing, not like a hotel | Not covered | Interior FAQ, Q15 |
| Several intermediaries in one booking | The party with the direct and final contract with the client files | Interior FAQ, Q10 |

The register applies to all types of lodging in Spanish territory (Interior FAQ Q13).

## 2. Who signs, and who answers for the data

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who signs the parte de entrada | Every person over 14 who uses the lodging. For under 14, the data are given by the adult accompanying them | art. 4.2 |
| Who supplies the parte | The establishment, which answers for the data matching the identity documents shown by the guests | art. 4.3 |
| Non-professional hosts | Exempt from the registry and retention duties of art. 5, not from the communication duties of art. 6 | art. 5.4 |

The decree says nothing more on whether a non-professional host still collects a signed parte under
art. 4. Say that this was not checked and ask the host.

## 3. Form, electronic signing and the register

- The register is electronic by law (art. 5.1). Retention: three years from the end of the service
  (art. 5.3).
- The decree does not say which kind of signature the guest uses and does not say an electronic
  signature is accepted. It leaves the "system and model" to be set (art. 4.2). No published model of
  the parte was found.
- Interior's FAQ accepts collecting the data by any system that lets the establishment verify their
  accuracy, even without the guest being physically present, if it complies with data protection (Q18);
  it accepts electronic devices for remote verification (Q19). It does not say "electronic signature on
  the parte". Claim no more than that.
- Say it plainly to the host: the signed parte is a record; whether Interior is satisfied with a
  remotely signed one is not stated, only remote data collection is.

## 4. The filing is a separate act

Within 24 hours of the booking or contract (or its cancellation) and of the start of service, the
guest and contract data must be sent to the authorities by a telematic procedure (art. 6.3, 6.4); only
non-professional hosts may use non-telematic means. Interior's electronic procedure is used (FAQ Q20),
and a third party may do it if the obligated party authorises it (Q22). Any change of data is a new
communication (Q26).

This skill prepares the signed record. It does not file, and it does not state when the filing became
enforceable (a date seen in vendor blogs was not found in any official text). The mandate (document 3)
can record the authorisation for a manager to file.

Sanctions: missing registers or omitted communications are serious (art. 8.2); irregular entries and late
communications are minor (art. 8.3). Fines in LO 4/2015 art. 39.1: serious 601 to 30,000 EUR, minor 100
to 600 EUR.

## 5. Annex I: the closed list of fields (Spanish, verbatim)

Guest data for professional operation (Annex I, A, point 3), the list printed in block C of document 1:

> Nombre. Primer apellido. Segundo apellido. Sexo. Numero de documento de identidad. Número de soporte
> del documento. Tipo de documento (DNI, pasaporte, TIE). Nacionalidad. Fecha de nacimiento. Lugar de
> residencia habitual: Dirección completa; Localidad; País. Teléfono fijo. Teléfono móvil. Correo
> electrónico. Número de viajeros. Relación de parentesco entre los viajeros (en el caso de que alguno
> sea menor de edad).

For non-professional operation (Annex I, B, point 3) the list is the same except that "Número de soporte
del documento" does not appear.

Transaction data (A, point 4 and B, point 4):

> Datos del contrato: Número de referencia; Fecha; Firmas. Datos de la ejecución del contrato: Fecha y
> hora de entrada; Fecha y hora de salida. Datos del inmueble (A): Dirección completa; Número de
> habitaciones; Conexión a Internet (si/no). Datos del pago: Tipo (efectivo, tarjeta de crédito,
> plataforma de pago, transferencia...); Identificación del medio de pago: tipo de tarjeta y número, IBAN
> cuenta bancaria, solución de pago por móvil, otros; Titular del medio de pago; Fecha de caducidad de la
> tarjeta; Fecha del pago.

Annex I also lists data of the operator (A, point 1: name or company, CIF or NIF, municipality,
province, telephone, e-mail, website, URL of the listing) and of the establishment (A, point 2: type,
name, full address, postal code, locality and province); for a non-professional host, the owner and the
property (B, points 1 and 2). These come from the host and are printed in block A, not asked of guests.

Observations: the signature is listed under the contract data ("Firmas"), not inside the traveller
block. Interior's FAQ says (Q27 to Q30) the DNI is mandatory from age 14 for Spanish citizens, the
e-mail can be replaced by a mobile number, and sex is required when it appears on the document.

Default for payment data: the list includes it. Ask the host whether it is gathered on this form or
taken from the booking record, and do not collect a payment card number on the signed form unless the
host says it is needed.

Identity picture: nothing in the texts read here requires an image of the ID. The Spanish data
protection authority's position was seen only in secondary sources and is not stated. Keep the default
of no picture.

## 6. Regional check-in documents (tourist and vacation homes only)

| Region | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|

The completed, signed copy is kept by the operator for one year and counts as evidence and as a contract. Users also show their ID for the parte de entrada | Decreto 28/2016 art. 7.1 to 7.3 |
| Canarias, vacation homes | If the booking was not already in writing, both parties sign before occupation a document with the extracted conditions, hours, maximum number of occupants and prices with IGIC included, in at least Spanish and English | Decreto 113/2015 art. 12.4 |

Andalucia: the text read is the February 2024 consolidation; later changes were not checked. Neither
rule prescribes a type of signature. Canarias: Ley 6/2025 repealed other articles of the same decree,
and its effect on art. 12.4 was not read, so say the rule was not confirmed as current. These rules are
for tourist and vacation homes, not hotels. Cataluna, Illes Balears, Comunitat Valenciana and Madrid:
not checked; do not state a rule for them.

## 7. Short-let registry (context only)

The Supreme Court annulled, in three judgments (19 May, 21 May and 1 June 2026), the single registry
procedure and the obligation to obtain a state registration number to offer short lets on platforms.
The rest of RD 1312/2024 stands, including the digital gateway for platform data (art. 7). Sanctions for
platforms under Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 are in Real Decreto-ley 29/2026, confirmed by Congress on 12
November 2026, and fall on platforms, not hosts. Regional tourism registers were not affected by the
judgments. Do not say that RD 1312/2024 was annulled as a whole. The status of Orden VAU/1560/2025 was
not verified. The procedure is not part of this skill.

## 8. Signature

Neither RD 933/2021 nor the regional texts above ask for a qualified electronic signature or a notary,
and none of them says an electronic signature is accepted. If the host asks, say what section 3 says.
